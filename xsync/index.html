Private beta · 0.39.0 (build 71)

XSync

Sync camera footage and production sound, review matches, and export synced clips and multicams to Final Cut Pro.

Apple Silicon · macOS 14 or later

Download on Google Drive

48 MB DMG. Sign in with the Google account you were invited with.

Install and activate

  1. Open the DMG, read START HERE, and drag XSync into Applications. If Finder offers to replace an existing copy, choose another destination.
  2. The beta is ad-hoc signed and hasn’t been notarized. If macOS blocks it, follow the Apple first-launch instructions for the copy you received.
  3. In XSync, open Beta license… and enter your invitation code while online.

Access lasts 30 days from first activation. Offline access lasts up to seven days within that term.
